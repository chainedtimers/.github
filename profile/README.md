### What is Chained Timers?

Standard smartphone timers only let you set one countdown at a time. Stopwatch apps force you to manually reset between intervals.

Chained Timers chains countdown segments together sequentially, vibrating and announcing segment transitions automatically:

- **Sequential Countdown Chains:** Build custom interval sequences (e.g. HIIT workouts, Wim Hof / box breathing, multi-step tea brewing, Pomodoro cycles).
- **Audio & Haptic Alerts:** Clear beeps, text-to-speech cues, and distinctive vibration pulses signal every interval boundary.
- **Offline & Installable PWA:** Runs offline with zero latency and instant start once installed to your home screen.
- **Native Android Shell:** Foreground service execution ensuring background accuracy even with the phone locked in your pocket.
